### Objective

Express amoebius own gates as workflow values, so the calculus is exercised by every gate run.

### Deliverables

- Each phase gate as a workflow value with its five arms declared.
- Evidence as the observation arm's output rather than a side effect.
- Teardown obligations discharged by the gate's own exit.
- A derivation from a gate declaration to its runnable form.

### Validation

A gate expressed as a value must produce the same verdict as the authored gate it replaces, and must leak no resource.

### Remaining Work

None in this phase. The three descriptive-only contracts (Phases 73, 77 and 89) own no runnable gate yet and
therefore cannot be routed; their owning phases must add a command before they can open. Live mechanism
behavior remains at each mechanism's declared register and is not reclassified as a Phase-49 result.
