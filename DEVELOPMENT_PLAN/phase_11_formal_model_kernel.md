result. The target is to represent a transition system once as a Haskell value, interpret and
explore it in-process, and lazily render any TLA+/CFG representation beneath `.build/**` without
treating generated bytes as an oracle.

`.hs`. Except for the `pb/**` bootstrap, no non-`.hs` behavioral source, fixture, oracle, or mutant may be
tracked. Any foreign representation, rendered specification, compiler transcript, suite manifest, generated

behavior only. It cannot use host, hardware, live-service, or cluster observations to make its claim pass.
